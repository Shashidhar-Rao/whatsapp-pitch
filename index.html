WhatsApp: The Execution Layer

A Builder-Operator Proposal for Kunal Shah

Executive Summary

You shared a clear vision for WhatsApp yesterday in your tweet. WhatsApp is already a shared language for three billion people. However, communication is currently separated from execution. Users talk about tasks but must leave the application to complete them.

I mapped out ways to embed context directly into conversations without adding visual clutter. This proposal outlines a strict product strategy to turn chat into an execution layer. We focus on low-risk services, maintaining the minimalist UI, and protecting the privacy moat.

The architecture allows Meta to directly attack the lucrative B2B advertising monopoly and build a high-margin token economy. It achieves this while keeping the chat inbox completely ad-free and operationally lightweight.

Product Core

  • AI-driven Intent Capture via Voice/Text
  • Token-based B2B Lead Generation
  • Live Shared Applets (Split Bills, Checklists)

The Hard "NOs"

  • No WeChat-style "Discover" tabs
  • No human-arbitrated fulfillment or dispute resolution
  • No in-thread advertising or cloud-processed intimacy

Services Marketplace

The Product

A conversational search engine powered by Meta AI. Users do not browse a directory. They speak their intent ("I need a CA in Bengaluru"). The AI structures a job brief and broadcasts it to verified professionals.

Customer Insight

Currently, finding B2B services on WhatsApp lacks interoperability. A blank search bar causes cognitive dissonance. Asking an AI feels like texting a concierge.

The Tradeoff (Tokens)

To prevent off-platform cash leakage, WhatsApp charges the business a token *to quote*, not a commission on the final job. This protects margins but requires businesses to pre-purchase credits.

Contextual Utility

The Product

"Live Shared Applets." Users tap a magic icon on receipts to instantly parse data (via on-device OCR) into interactive split-bill widgets natively embedded in the chat.

Customer Insight

Aggressive auto-invoicing ruins social vibes. Users need a "Draft Mode" to edit splits privately before publishing them to the group.

The Flywheel

Contextual expense sharing is the ultimate Trojan Horse for WhatsApp Pay, reducing settlement friction to zero.

Agentic Networking

Connecting Meta's public social graph (Threads) to private WhatsApp execution via a native, opt-in Professional Card.

The Contextual Trigger

Instead of a cold settings menu, the AI drops a private, ephemeral message into dormant professional chats offering to silently monitor Threads for relevant icebreakers based on specific chosen tags.

Don't: Blanket tracking. Provide a "Glass Box" UI where users toggle exactly which connections the AI is allowed to monitor.

The Ecosystem Unlock (B2B Ads)

The ultimate commercial value is not just inside WhatsApp—it is the data bridge it builds for the rest of Meta.

Disrupting LinkedIn

The Professional Card generates pristine zero-party B2B data (Role, Industry, Intent). With explicit opt-in, this data feeds Meta's ad algorithm, allowing highly targeted B2B SaaS and Real Estate ads on Instagram.

The Result: A massive ROI funnel without a single ad in the WhatsApp chat UI.
